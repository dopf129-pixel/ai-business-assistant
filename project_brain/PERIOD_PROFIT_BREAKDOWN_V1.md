# Period Profit Breakdown v1

Builds a read-only explanation layer over Period Profit Summary v1.

It exposes total revenue, Ozon net accrual, product cost, configured tax, profit and margin, and ranks products by period profit.

The breakdown preserves the summary completeness flags. In v1 returns, advertising and storage remain explicitly excluded, so ranking and total profit must be interpreted within the same scoped operational-profit definition.
